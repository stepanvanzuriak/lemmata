# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Lemmata to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code,
and may ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, use [a private security advisory](https://github.com/stepanvanzuriak/lemmata/security/advisories/new)
on the repository, so that only the maintainers see it. Reports are handled promptly and treated as confidential.
